Parkzoid Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Parkzoid ("Parkzoid", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Parkzoid mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Parkzoid does not require you to create an account, provide your name, phone number, or email address in order to play the game.

The game may use a unique player identifier generated for the game. This identifier is used to distinguish one game installation/player from another and to support game functionality.

Depending on how you use the game, the following information may be processed:

  • Unique player or game identifier
  • Game progress and gameplay-related information
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Device and app information required for functionality, diagnostics, and advertising
  • Advertising-related information handled by third-party advertising providers

2. Advertising

Parkzoid may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and information about interaction with advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's privacy documentation.

3. How We Use Information

Information associated with the game may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Parkzoid
  • Maintain game progress and gameplay functionality
  • Generate and manage a unique player identifier
  • Improve game performance and stability
  • Detect and resolve technical problems
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the game

4. Third-Party Services

Parkzoid may use third-party services that process information on our behalf or provide services required by the game.

These services may include Google Play services and Google AdMob. Third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage

Some game information may be stored locally on your device. Certain information required for game functionality or advertising may also be processed by third-party services.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant game functionality, maintain security, comply with legal requirements, or for other legitimate operational purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Parkzoid is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that is not appropriate, please contact us through the contact information available on the Parkzoid Google Play listing.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using Parkzoid at any time. You may also uninstall the game from your device. Information stored locally by the game may be removed when the application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of your device and any third-party services.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with Parkzoid. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Parkzoid, our services, or applicable legal requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Parkzoid's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Parkzoid Google Play listing.
