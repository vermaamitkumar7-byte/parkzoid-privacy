PARKZOID Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

PARKZOID is a car parking game published by the developer of PARKZOID. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the PARKZOID mobile application.

Important: PARKZOID does not require users to create an account or log in. The app does not maintain user profiles.

1. Information We Collect

PARKZOID does not directly collect or require users to provide their name, email address, phone number, password, profile photo, contacts, or precise physical location.

The app stores certain game-related information locally on the device, such as the player's in-game coin balance and selected car design. This information is used only to provide the game's functionality.

2. Advertising

PARKZOID uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interaction information, and diagnostic or technical information as necessary to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

Advertising data is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and settings.

3. In-App Purchases

PARKZOID uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases, including game coins and PARKZOID Pro.

Payment card, bank, or other payment credentials are handled by Google Play. PARKZOID does not receive or store users' complete payment credentials.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

PARKZOID may store game-related preferences and progress locally on your device. This includes information such as:

  • In-game coin balance
  • Selected car design
  • Other local game preferences required for gameplay

These local game values are not associated with a PARKZOID user account because PARKZOID does not provide user accounts.

5. Data Sharing

PARKZOID does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services required for advertising and Google Play billing. These services process data according to their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Deletion Requests

PARKZOID does not create user accounts or maintain a user account database. Therefore, there is normally no PARKZOID account to delete.

If you believe PARKZOID has processed personal information associated with you and you would like to request deletion of information under our control, you may contact us using the email address below.

Email: sanamappsupport@gmail.com

Please include "PARKZOID Data Deletion Request" in the subject line. We will review the request and delete applicable information under our control where required and applicable.

Data processed independently by third-party services such as Google may be subject to those services' own privacy controls and deletion procedures.

7. Children's Privacy

PARKZOID is not designed to require children to create accounts or submit personal information. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the app.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app. Data transmitted by the app uses network connections provided by the Android platform and the relevant third-party services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to PARKZOID, its features, or applicable requirements. The updated policy will be published on this page.
